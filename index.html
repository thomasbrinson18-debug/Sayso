What gets posted

  • Your stars, visit frequency and note. Notes are shown without any demographics next to them.

Your profile

Every question is optional. Skipped answers show up as “Not shared” in crowd charts.

How your answers are used

  • Your profile is private. Only your account can read it.
  • Crowds, not people. Ratings are only ever shown as group totals.
  • Small groups stay hidden. Any slice with fewer than 5 people is folded into “Smaller groups,” and places with fewer than that many ratings show no crowd at all.
  • Changing your profile applies to ratings you post from now on.
